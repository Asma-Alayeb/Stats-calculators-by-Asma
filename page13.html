Summary Statistics

Bar Chart

A bar chart displays the frequency of each category. The bars are separated because the categories are distinct.

Pie Chart

A pie chart shows the relative frequency of each category.

Histogram

A histogram displays the distribution of quantitative data. The bars touch because the intervals represent continuous numerical values.

Five-Number Summary

The five-number summary describes the center and spread of quantitative data.
